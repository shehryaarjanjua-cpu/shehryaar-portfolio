04 — About

Behind the timeline

I'm Shehryaar, a video editor and content creator based in Halalpur Kotmomin, Sargodha. Over the past two years I've focused on clean, modern edits — sharp pacing, contemporary transitions, and a color style built for platforms like YouTube and Instagram. I cut for brands, creators, and businesses across short-form, long-form, and documentary work.

Currently editing for CortexMD on YouTube, alongside ongoing work with BBC Consultants, HR Hotel Islamabad, and a handful of other clients. Most projects start with a call to understand pacing, audience, and platform, then move through a rough cut, a picture lock, and a final pass for color and sound.
